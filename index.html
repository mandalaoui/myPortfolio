      About Me

      Who I Am

      I build AI systems that run inside real businesses, and teach teams to use them.

      I'm an AI and full-stack engineer with a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Bar-Ilan University. I work forward-deployed style: directly with the business, from the first conversation to software running in production.

      My work covers the whole path: understanding how a business actually operates, designing the solution, building the frontend, backend and AI integrations, deploying it, and teaching the team to use it. Before tech, I served nearly four years as an HR officer in the IDF, leading people and delivering trainings and talks to groups of 5 to 200. That's still how I run projects and trainings today.
