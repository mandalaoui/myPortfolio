Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information this website (the portfolio of Omer Mandalaoui, Petah Tikva, Israel) collects, how it is used, and your choices. Questions: mandalaoui.omer@gmail.com.

1. What is collected

  • AI chat ("AI Omer"). The messages you type and the answers generated, with the time, the detected language and a technical classification of the message (for example its topic, or whether it was flagged as an attempt to misuse the chat). Please do not enter personal or sensitive information in the chat.
  • Usage limits. Your IP address is used briefly, in memory, to limit the number of chat messages. It is not saved with the conversation.
  • Language preference. Your choice of English or Hebrew is stored in your own browser (localStorage) and is not sent to me.
  • Information you send me. If you contact me by email or WhatsApp, or book a call, I receive the details you provide.
  • Hosting logs. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) under its own policy.

The site does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

2. How it is used

To answer chat questions, to improve the quality of the answers, to prevent abuse of the chat, and to reply to your inquiries. Information is not sold or used for advertising.

3. Service providers

  • Netlify: hosting of the site, the chat server function and the storage of chat records.
  • Groq: processes chat messages in order to generate the answers.
  • Google Fonts and code delivery networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg) and Cloudinary (project demo videos): deliver fonts, scripts and media, and receive your IP address when doing so.
  • Links to Google Calendar (booking a call), WhatsApp, GitHub and LinkedIn lead to services governed by their own privacy policies.

Some of these providers process information outside Israel, including in the United States.

4. Retention

Chat records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. Correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

5. Your rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 (and, where it applies, the EU GDPR), you may ask to access, correct or delete information about you. Write to mandalaoui.omer@gmail.com. Since the chat does not ask for your identity, please describe the conversation (date, approximate time and content) so it can be found.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect the information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
